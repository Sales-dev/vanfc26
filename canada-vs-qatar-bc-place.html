Canada vs Qatar at BC Place — June 18, 2026

Canada’s first World Cup match on home soil in Vancouver. Here is the kickoff time, what tickets cost, how to actually get into the stadium, and where to watch if you don’t have a seat.

This is the second of Canada’s group-stage matches — the men’s national team opens in Toronto on June 12, then comes west to Vancouver to face Qatar at BC Place on June 18. It’s one of two Canada home games in Vancouver (the other is Canada vs Switzerland on June 24), and it will be one of the loudest, most emotional days of the tournament in the city. The 3 PM afternoon kickoff means a long, busy build-up downtown.

Tickets & prices

The match was not sold out heading into tournament week. On June 9, unsold tickets through FIFA ran from about $770 to $2,625 CAD, with roughly 300 remaining; official and third-party resale showed some from around $706. Prices move fast — buy only through FIFA or its official resale.

How to get into the stadium

⚠️ It’s not a normal entry

BC Place has one spectator entrance (the Pacific Boulevard side), and Stadium-Chinatown Station is closed for stadium access. Take SkyTrain to Main Street–Science World and walk the secured “Last Mile” to the gate. For a 3 PM Canada kickoff, leave extra time — this will be a big, slow crowd.

No ticket? Where to watch in Vancouver

Near the stadium

Library Square & Shark Club are the closest high-energy pubs — book ahead for a Canada game, they will be full.

Free & outdoor

The Canada Soccer House at the Shipyards (North Van, free, SeaBus) shows every match on a huge outdoor screen — the best free Canada atmosphere.

Fan Festival

The free FIFA Fan Festival at the PNE has giant screens, food and music all tournament long.

What to bring

  • Clear bag only — max 12″×6″×12″; no backpacks or purses. It speeds up security.
  • Your digital ticket in your phone wallet, plus a charged phone.
  • It’s a warm sunny afternoon (~20°C) and the roof is closed — no need for rain gear, but bring water for the walk and queue.
  • Canada red. The atmosphere is the whole point.

FAQ

What time is Canada vs Qatar in Vancouver?

3:00 PM Pacific Time on Thursday, June 18, 2026 at BC Place (6:00 PM ET, 11:00 PM BST).

How much are Canada vs Qatar tickets?

On June 9, unsold tickets ran about $770–$2,625 CAD through FIFA, with roughly 300 left. Resale showed some from around $706. Buy only via FIFA or official resale.

How do I get into BC Place for this match?

Use Main Street–Science World SkyTrain (not Stadium-Chinatown) and walk the secured Last Mile to the only entrance on the Pacific Boulevard side. See our access guide.

Where can I watch if I don’t have a ticket?

Pubs near the stadium like Library Square and Shark Club, the free Canada Soccer House at the Shipyards in North Van, or the free FIFA Fan Festival at the PNE.
