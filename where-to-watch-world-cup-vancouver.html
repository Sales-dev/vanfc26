Where to Watch the World Cup in Vancouver

No ticket, or just want the atmosphere? Vancouver is one of the best football-watching cities in North America. Here are the free outdoor screens, the biggest watch parties, and the bars where each nation gathers — plus how to pick the right spot for your match.

Free & outdoor — the big screens

If you want the festival feeling for nothing, start here. These are the marquee free viewing sites for the whole tournament.

Canada Soccer House — The Shipyards

North Van · free · SeaBus
464 sq ft screenEvery matchOutdoor festival

Canada Soccer’s official fan site — a huge outdoor screen on the waterfront with live music, food and merch. The best free Canada atmosphere in the region. Take the SeaBus from Waterfront (12 min).

FIFA Fan Festival — PNE

Hastings Park · free
Giant screensJune 11–July 19Food & music

The official FIFA Fan Festival: every match on giant screens with entertainment all tournament long. Full Fan Festival guide →

Granville Street pedestrian zone

Downtown · free
June 11–July 19Patios & barsLive music

Five car-free blocks of patios, vendors and big-screen energy in the heart of downtown — the most festival-like street in the city on match days.

Closest to BC Place

Pre- or post-match, the stadium-district pubs are the highest energy in the city. Library Square (Arsenal’s home, $5 pints, 22-ft screen) and the Shark Club (two 12-ft screens, book ahead) are the go-tos. Note the match-day access changes — see our getting-in guide. By neighbourhood, quickly

Commercial Drive is the soul of Vancouver football — if you could go anywhere with no ticket, go here. Gastown for expat pub culture, Yaletown for the best screens (Red Card), Davie/West End for a neighbourhood feel (Score on Davie), Main Street for craft beer locals, and Kitsilano if you’re on the west side. Every venue, by area, is on the bars & pubs guide.

How to pick

  • Bringing kids or on a budget? Canada Soccer House at the Shipyards or the Fan Festival — both free and outdoor.
  • Want the loudest crowd for a specific nation? Go to that team’s home base above.
  • Watching several games back-to-back? Red Card in Yaletown.
  • Pre-game before walking to BC Place? Library Square or Shark Club downtown.

FAQ

Where can I watch the World Cup for free in Vancouver?

The Canada Soccer House at the Shipyards (North Van), the FIFA Fan Festival at the PNE, and the Granville Street pedestrian zone all show matches free and outdoors.

Where do Canada fans watch in Vancouver?

Library Square Public House downtown (closest to BC Place) and the free Canada Soccer House outdoor screen at the Shipyards in North Vancouver.

What’s the best bar to watch multiple matches?

Red Card in Yaletown — 16+ TVs and two projector screens, built to run several games at once.
